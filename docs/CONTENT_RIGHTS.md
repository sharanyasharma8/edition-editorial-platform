# Content, imagery and rights notes

- All story copy and author profiles in the seed dataset are fictional demonstration material written for EDITION. They must not be represented as real reporting or real contributors.
- Seed hero/gallery images currently use remote Unsplash image URLs. The source URL and a demonstration credit do **not** establish a publication licence or photographer permission. Confirm the current Unsplash licence, any model/property releases and the actual photographer/agency credit for each image before public or commercial use. Replace any asset without clear rights.
- The CMS stores `heroAlt` and `imageCredit` separately. Alt text should describe visible content, not speculate about identity, intent or protected traits. Credits should identify the photographer, agency, rights holder or explicit licence as appropriate.
- Uploaded images are restricted to JPEG, PNG and WebP, validated by MIME type and file signature, and capped at 5 MB. This technical validation does not grant rights to publish the file.
- AI-generated image descriptions and editorial suggestions are drafts. A human editor must inspect the actual image and approve the wording.
